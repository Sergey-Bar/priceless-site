Trust Center

What PRICELESS actually does today, one capability at a time — and what it does not do. A capability marked available is reachable and carries no open blocker; whether a certified source stands behind it is the separate column beside it. Everything else is listed as it is, not as it is planned.

What is certified, and what is not

Each row is one capability in the product's capability registry. The vocabulary of statuses is closed and mechanically enforced: a capability that is not fully available must declare at least one blocker, and a capability declared available may not have any. A blocker is the concrete work that would change the status. The last column names what is missing, in one sentence; the registry that ships with the product holds the full engineering text of every blocker.

A route being reachable says nothing about whether there is a certified store behind it. This column is the measured answer: PRICELESS records no certified source today, so every capability that reads a store says so, rather than presenting an empty answer as a working one.

How fresh a price is

Every price is shown with the store it came from and the time it was observed. A price is used in a comparison, or sent as an alert, only when it is recent, its shipping and tax are both known, its condition is known to be new, and the source's certification is current. When a price does not clear those checks, PRICELESS says the price is unknown rather than showing a number it cannot stand behind. A price for something that is out of stock is not a price you can buy, so it does not raise an alert either.

Which store a price came from

A price is shown only from a source whose configuration version matches the version the observation was taken with and whose certification for that product category has not expired. PRICELESS holds no agreement with the stores it reads, and says so rather than implying one. A store being configured is not the same as a store being certified, and neither is market coverage. When PRICELESS cannot attribute a price to a source that passes those checks, it withholds the price rather than showing it unattributed.

What is not built

Basket comparison across stores, travel, grocery, subscriptions and payment-method cost, cashback, affiliate links, Telegram notifications, and shared or family lists are not available. They appear above as not available rather than as coming soon. Shared and family lists are not even scaffolded: there is no route, no stored list and no screen, so there is nothing to switch off and nothing waiting behind a flag.

Reporting a price that is wrong

If a price PRICELESS shows is wrong, out of date, or belongs to a different product, we want to know. In the extension you can report it from the store's row in the comparison. You can also write to support@priceless.ai
