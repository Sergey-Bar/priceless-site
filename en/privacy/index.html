Privacy

Welcome to PRICELESS. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when using our browser extension and related services. Use of PRICELESS constitutes acceptance of the terms described below.

1. Information We Collect

PRICELESS is designed for privacy by default. It collects the minimum information needed for a user-requested comparison:

  • Installation ID (Install ID): a random identifier used for local settings and watchlists. It is not linked to your personal identity.
  • User-requested comparison data: when you click PRICELESS, the product identifier and comparison request are sent to the PRICELESS API. PRICELESS does not monitor tabs or collect browsing history in the background.
  • Account information: If you choose to create a user account, we will collect your email address. This information is used solely for account management and syncing data between devices.
  • Crash data (Telemetry): We use Sentry for error and crash monitoring. This information includes technical data about your browser and operating system, without personally identifiable information.

2. What We Don't Collect

It's important to clarify what PRICELESS does NOT do:

  • We do not collect a background browsing history; the product page is read only after you click PRICELESS.
  • We do not collect passwords, credit card information, or financial data.
  • We do not sell your information to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • We do not use tracking pixels or third-party cookies for advertising purposes.

3. Use of Information

We use the collected information only for the following purposes:

  • Providing real-time price comparison and coupon services.
  • Sending price drop notifications (if you've signed up for them).
  • Providing and improving the comparison results you request.
  • Technical support and resolving issues reported by users.

4. Data Security and Retention

We implement advanced technical and organizational security measures to protect your information. Price data is cached locally in your browser for fast performance. Price history data is kept in an aggregated form on our servers after 90 days to maintain efficiency and privacy.

5. Your Rights and Deletion Requests

We believe in transparency and full user control over their information. Under Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 1981, you have the right to review information held about you, request correction or deletion. PRICELESS allows you to perform the following actions simply:

  • Local deletion: You can delete all locally stored data in your browser (cache, product history, and installation ID) at any time through the extension settings with one click.
  • Account deletion: If you chose to create a user account, you can request its complete deletion from our servers. Such a request will result in the deletion of your email address and all associated watchlists.
  • Information request: You can contact us at any time to receive a detailed report on the anonymous data associated with your installation ID.

6. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect technological changes, legal requirements, or service improvements. Whenever a significant change is made, we will update the "last updated" date at the bottom of the page and send a notification within the extension to existing users. We recommend reviewing this policy regularly to stay informed about how we protect your information.

7. Contact

For any questions about your data privacy at PRICELESS, you can contact our data protection officer at: support@priceless.ai.

Last updated: May 2026. Our headquarters are in Israel, and the service is subject to the laws of the State of Israel.
