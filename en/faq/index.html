Frequently asked questions

What is PRICELESS?

A price checking tool — add the extension, open a product page, click PRICELESS, and see if the price is right.

Is PRICELESS free?

Yes. No payment and no mandatory registration.

Where do the prices come from?

After you click PRICELESS on a product page, the requested comparison reads that tab once. Results include the retailer source and check time when known.

How do coupons work?

When a coupon is available, it appears in the results. Copy and use on the store's website.

What's the difference between guests and accounts?

Guests can compare prices and keep a watchlist on this device. An account enables optional watchlist sync and alerts across devices.

How do I get notifications?

Alerts are optional. On the website, sign in, add a product, and set a target price; an account is required to sync and receive them across devices.

Does PRICELESS work on mobile?

The website works as a PWA in a mobile browser. The Chrome extension is available on desktop.

What information do you collect?

The product page is read only after you click PRICELESS. An anonymous installation ID and optional technical diagnostics support the service; we do not collect a background browsing history.

Do you earn from links?

Not yet. No affiliate programme is running, so no link in PRICELESS pays us. When one is, every such link will be labeled, and it will not change which price is shown or how it is ranked.
